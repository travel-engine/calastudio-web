# Waitlist

If a class is full, the booking page shows **Join waitlist** instead of **Book**. No payment is taken when you join — you're only charged (or a credit is used) once a spot actually opens up and you claim it.

## Joining

1. Click **Join waitlist** on a full class
2. Sign in if you haven't already
3. If the studio allows guests, choose how many you're waiting with — you'll only be offered a spot once enough room opens up for your whole group
4. Click **Join the waitlist**

You'll see your position in line ("You're #3 on the waitlist") and get an email confirming you've joined. You can check your position anytime from **My waitlist** in your account.

## Getting promoted

When a spot frees up — someone cancels, or doesn't claim their own hold in time — the studio's system offers it to the next eligible person in line:

- **Most of the time:** one member at a time gets a private, time-limited hold (typically a couple of hours). You'll get an email the moment it's your turn.
- **Close to the class start time:** everyone still waiting gets notified at once, and it's first-come, first-served.
- **Right before the class starts:** freed spots stop being offered to the waitlist at all — the studio may fill it as a walk-in instead.

Either way, once you're offered a spot it shows up in **My waitlist** as **Spot ready**, with a deadline to claim it. Click **Claim my spot** before it expires to turn it into a real booking — that's when payment or your pass credit is actually taken. If you let the hold expire, it moves on to the next person and you drop back to waiting.

## Leaving

Go to **My waitlist** and click **Leave waitlist** — any time, no penalty. This also applies if you've already been offered a spot and decide you don't want it after all.

## If a spot opens up before you even join

If you try to join the waitlist for a class that actually has room (someone cancelled right as you were looking), you'll be offered the normal booking flow instead — there's no point queuing for a spot that's already available.
