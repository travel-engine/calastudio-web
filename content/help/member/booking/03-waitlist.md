# Waitlist

Waitlisting isn't available yet. Right now, once a session reaches capacity it simply won't let you book — you'll see a message that it's full rather than an option to join a queue for it.

If you're after a specific full class, your best bet today is to check back on the booking page — a spot opens up as soon as someone already booked cancels theirs.
