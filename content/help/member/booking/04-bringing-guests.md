# Bringing guests

If your studio allows guests, you can bring a friend along. Your guest doesn't need an account.

## When you book

Before confirming, choose how many guests you're bringing. You'll only see as many as there are spots left.

## Adding a guest to a booking you've already made

1. Go to **My account → My bookings**
2. On the class, tap **+ Add a guest**
3. If you have more than one pass that can pay for guests, choose which one under **Pay with**
4. Choose how many guests, then tap **Add**

Each guest uses credits from your pass, usually 1 per guest.

If none of your passes can pay for another guest, you'll see the passes you can buy instead. Tap **Buy**, pay by card, and your guest is added straight away.

## Removing a guest

Tap **Remove** next to the guest and confirm. If you remove them before the studio's cancellation window, the credit goes back to your pass. Later than that, it's a late cancellation and the credit may not come back.

Cancelling your own booking cancels your guests too.

## How many guests can I bring?

Your studio decides. It can differ between classes and between passes, so you might be able to bring more guests to some classes than others.
