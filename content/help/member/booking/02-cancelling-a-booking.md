# Cancelling a booking

## How to cancel

1. Go to **My bookings**
2. Find the session you want to cancel
3. Click **Cancel booking**

The cancel button is only shown if you're within the cancellation window. If it's not visible, the window has passed.

## Cancellation window

Each studio sets their own cancellation window — for example, "cancel up to 12 hours before the session". If you cancel inside this window, you get your credit or payment back. If you cancel outside this window, the credit is forfeited.

## What happens to your credit

| When you cancel | What happens |
|-----------------|-------------|
| Inside the cancellation window | Credit returned to your pass, or card payment refunded |
| Outside the cancellation window | Credit forfeited (no refund) |

Refunds to your card take 3–7 business days to appear.

## Late cancel

If you cancel outside the window, your booking shows as **Late cancel** in your history. Some studios charge a late cancellation fee — you'll be notified if this applies.

## Studio cancels your session

If the studio cancels a session you're booked into, your credit is always returned regardless of the cancellation window — no exceptions.
