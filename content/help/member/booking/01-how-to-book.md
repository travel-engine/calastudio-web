# How to book a session

## Find a session

Browse your studio's booking page to find a session you'd like to attend. You can see:
- The session name, day, and time
- How many spots are remaining
- The price

## Book

1. Click on a session to see the full details
2. Click **Book**
3. If you're not signed in, you'll be asked to create an account or sign in
4. If it's your first time, you may need to accept the studio's terms and liability waiver before continuing
5. If the studio allows guests on this session, choose how many you're bringing
6. Choose how you'd like to pay — with an existing pass, by buying a new pass, or as a one-off drop-in by card
7. Click **Confirm booking**

You'll receive a confirmation email within 30 seconds.

## Paying with a pass

If you have an active pass with credits, it will be shown as a payment option. The credit is deducted from your pass when you confirm.

## Paying by card

If you don't have a pass, you can pay the drop-in price by card. Your payment details are handled securely by Stripe — CalaStudio never sees your card number.

## After booking

Your booking appears in **My bookings**. If you booked an online session, your join link will either be in the confirmation email or sent 15 minutes before the session starts.
