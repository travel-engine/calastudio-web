# Paying for a pass the studio added

Sometimes your studio adds a pass to your account and lets you pay for it later. You can book classes with it straight away.

## Pay from the email

If the studio sent you a payment reminder, open the email and tap **Pay**. You don't need to sign in. Enter your card details and confirm. Your payment is handled securely by Stripe.

When it's done you'll see a confirmation and get a receipt by email. If you open the link again later, it tells you the pass is already paid.

## Pay from your account

1. Sign in on your studio's booking page and open **My account**
2. Under **My passes**, an unpaid pass shows **Unpaid** with the amount
3. Tap **Pay**, enter your card details and confirm

It can take a minute for the pass to show as paid.

## Paying another way

You can also pay the studio directly, for example in cash or by bank transfer. The studio then marks the pass as paid.
