# Auto-renewal

Auto-renewal keeps you from losing access to your favourite sessions when a pass runs out — it's optional, and only available on passes your studio has enabled it for.

## How it works

When an eligible pass is close to expiring, CalaStudio automatically issues you a new pass and charges the card on file — no action needed from you. The new pass usually replaces the old one on the same pass type, unless your studio has set it to renew into a different one.

## Turning it on or off

1. Go to **My passes**
2. Open the pass you want to change
3. Toggle **Auto-renew**

You can change this at any time, including right after you buy a pass.

## If a renewal fails

If your card is declined, we'll try again and notify you by email. Your current pass keeps working until it actually expires — a failed renewal never cuts off access mid-pass. Update your payment method from **My account** to fix a failed renewal.

## Not every pass supports it

Auto-renewal is a setting on the pass type itself — if you don't see the toggle, your studio hasn't enabled it for that pass. Fixed-date passes (ones that expire on a specific calendar date rather than X days after purchase) generally aren't good candidates for auto-renewal and are often excluded.
