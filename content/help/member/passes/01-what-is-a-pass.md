# What is a pass?

A pass is a bundle of credits you buy upfront and use to book sessions. It's cheaper than paying drop-in for each class.

## Types of passes

| Type | How it works |
|------|-------------|
| **Credit pack** | A fixed number of sessions. e.g. 10-class pass. |
| **Unlimited** | Book as many sessions as you like within a date range. |

## Expiry

Every pass has an expiry date. Credits not used by the expiry date are forfeited. Your studio sets the expiry — common options are 30 days, 90 days, or a fixed calendar date.

## Checking your balance

Go to **My passes** to see all your active passes, credits remaining, and expiry dates.

## Auto-renewal

Some passes can be set to auto-renew. When your pass is close to expiring, it will automatically renew and charge your saved card — so you never lose access.

You can turn auto-renewal on or off at any time from **My passes**.
