# Notification preferences

There's no settings screen to control these yet — every member gets the same set of automatic emails, with no way to opt out individually.

## What you'll get

- A confirmation when you book a session
- A reminder the day before, and again about an hour before it starts
- The join link for an online session, sent shortly before it begins
- A receipt after any card payment
- A heads-up if your pass is about to expire
- A notice if the studio cancels a session you're booked into

All of these go to the email address on your account.
