# Add your studio to your home screen

You can add your studio's booking site to your phone's home screen. It gets its own icon, opens full-screen like an app, and goes straight to the class schedule. There's nothing to download from the App Store or Google Play.

The quickest way: after you book a class, or on your **My account** page, tap **Install app** (Android) or **Show me how** (iPhone).

## On an Android phone (Chrome)

1. Open your studio's booking site in Chrome.
2. Tap **Install app** if you see it on the page. Otherwise, tap the **⋮** menu at the top right, then **Install** (some phones say **Add to Home screen**).
3. Tap **Install** to confirm.

## On an iPhone or iPad (Safari)

1. Open your studio's booking site in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up) in Safari's toolbar.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add**.

## Good to know

- **It's the same account.** Your bookings, passes and memberships are the same as on the website. You may need to sign in once inside the app.
- **One app per studio.** If you book at more than one CalaStudio studio, each one is added separately, with its own icon.
- **Removing it:** press and hold the icon, then choose **Remove** or **Uninstall**, like any other app.
- **Don't want to see the suggestion?** Tap **✕** on it. It won't show again on that phone for 30 days.
