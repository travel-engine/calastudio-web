# Inviting teachers

Add your team members so they can manage their own sessions and students.

## Invite a teacher

1. Go to **Team** in the sidebar
2. Click **+ Invite teacher**
3. Enter their name, email address, and select a role
4. Click **Add to team**

They'll appear in your team list immediately. When they sign up with the same email address, they'll have access to your studio.

## Teacher roles

| Role | What they can do |
|------|-----------------|
| **Super teacher** | Manages sessions and members. Can issue passes and process bookings. Cannot change billing or studio settings. |
| **Teacher** | Views and manages their own sessions and students only. |
| **Basic teacher** | Marks attendance only. Cannot see member details. |
| **Front desk** | Views the schedule and checks in members. No financial access. |

## Multiple roles

A person can hold more than one role. For example, a senior member who covers classes temporarily can be both a **Member** and a **Basic teacher** at the same time. You can add or remove roles at any time from their profile.

## Multi-studio teachers

If a teacher already has a CalaStudio account (from another studio), they don't need to create a new one. Just invite them with their existing email — they'll see your studio added to their account automatically.
