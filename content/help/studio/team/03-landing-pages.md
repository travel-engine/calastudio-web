# Teacher landing pages

Every teacher gets their own public page at `{your-studio}.calastudio.app/teachers/{their-slug}` — a bio, photos, and their upcoming sessions that you can link to directly or share on social media.

## Editing a profile

1. Go to **Team** and click **Edit** on a teacher
2. Under **Landing page profile**, fill in:
   - **Display name** — how their name appears on the page
   - **Tagline** — one line shown under their name
   - **Bio** — full biography (Markdown supported)
   - **Specialties** and **Certifications** — comma-separated lists
   - **Years of experience**
   - **Instagram** and **website** links
3. Click **Save changes** at the bottom of the page

## Adding photos

Under **Photos**, click **Upload photo** (JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 5 MB) or paste an image URL and click **Add**. Up to 10 photos.

The first photo is the **Hero** at the top of their page; the rest appear in a gallery below the bio. Click **Make hero** on any other photo to move it to the front, or the **×** on a photo to remove it. Photo changes are saved with **Save changes** like everything else.

## Publishing

A teacher's page isn't public until you turn it on. Under **Visibility**:

- **Publish landing page** — makes the page publicly visible at its URL. Leave this off while you're still setting it up.
- **Show in studio teacher directory** — includes the teacher on your main studio page's teacher listing, separate from whether their own page is published.

You can switch either independently — for example, publish a teacher's page but leave them out of the directory if you're not ready to promote them yet. Once published, a **View landing page** button appears at the top of their page.

## Primary teacher

Click **Make primary** to show a teacher first in your studio's teacher directory. There's one per studio, so this takes effect straight away and clears it from whoever had it before.

## Checking published vs. draft

The **Team** list shows each teacher's landing page as **Published** or **Draft**, and whether they're **Shown** or **Hidden** on your studio page, so you can see who's live without opening every profile. The primary teacher has a **Lead** tag.
