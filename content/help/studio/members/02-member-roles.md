# Member roles

Everyone who signs up through your booking page gets the **Member** role automatically — it's what lets them book sessions, buy and use passes, and manage their own account. There's nothing to configure here; it's the baseline every member starts with.

## Staff roles are separate

Admin, Super teacher, Teacher, Basic teacher, and Front desk are a different set of roles, for people who help run the studio rather than just attend it — see **Team → Teacher roles** for what each one can do. They're granted from **Team**, not from anywhere in Members.

## Holding both at once

A person isn't limited to one or the other. A student who also teaches a weekly class is both a **Member** (they book and pay like anyone else) and, say, a **Basic teacher** (they can run their own roster) on the same account — see **Team → Inviting teachers → Multiple roles** for how that's set up.
