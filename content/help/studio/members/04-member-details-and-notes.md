# Member details and notes

## Finding members

**Members** in the sidebar lists everyone at your studio. Search by name or email, and click a column heading (**Name**, **Email** or **Last active**) to sort. Click it again to reverse the order. On a phone, use the **Sort** menu next to the search box.

Sorting by **Last active** puts the most recently active members first. Members who haven't signed in yet always sit at the bottom.

## A member's page

Click a member to see their passes, memberships, orders, upcoming bookings, details and notes. Opening a member's page needs the permission to view members; by default that's admins and Super teachers.

## Editing details

Click **Edit details** to change a member's name, phone, date of birth, emergency contact, address, person type (adult, senior, student, child) and gender. Leading and trailing spaces are removed automatically.

Two things you can't change here:

- **Email.** It's how the member signs in, so only they can change it, from their account.
- **Name and contact details of someone who also belongs to another studio.** These are shared across every studio they belong to, so only the member can change them. The form shows them read-only. You can still set their person type and gender, which are just for your studio.

Every change is recorded in the member's activity. By default, admins and Super teachers can edit member details.

## Notes

The **Notes** box on a member's page is for staff only; members never see it. Type a note and click **Save note**. Each note is dated and shows who wrote it, newest first.

Notes can't be edited or removed once saved, so they're a reliable record. To correct something, add a new note.

CalaStudio adds a note automatically when a welcome email is sent, including any credits carried over from your old system. These show "CalaStudio" as the author.

Notes are visible to admins only.
