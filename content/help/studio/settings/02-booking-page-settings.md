# Booking page settings

Go to **Settings** and open the **Booking page** tab to change how your public booking page at `{your-studio}.calastudio.app` looks. Class times, prices and teacher bios come from your schedule, passes and team — this tab is for your branding and the words and photos around them.

As on the Studio tab, **each card saves on its own** with its own **Save** and **Discard**.

## Branding

- **Logo** — click **Upload logo** (or **Replace logo**). Images must be under 5 MB
- **Brand colour** — the accent colour used on your public pages. Use the colour picker or type a hex code like `#085041`

## Page style

Seven styles for the same page — each card previews the look. Click one, then **Save**. **Photo First** and **Photo First Light** open with one full-screen photograph (your Hero photo) with your studio name over it; the dark one keeps the whole page dark, the light one switches to light pages below the photo. Your logo and brand colour carry across every style, and you can switch any time.

## Page content

The words and photos on your home page, as a list of rows. Each row shows a short summary (for example the headline, or "12 photos"); click **Edit** to open its fields and **Close** to fold it away. When you're done, click **Save** on the Page content card — one save covers every row.

| Row | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Hero** | Tagline, headline, an emphasised word in the headline, intro paragraph, and the label for the hero's second button |
| **Hero photo** | The big photo at the top. Portrait works best |
| **About** | Heading, text (leave a blank line between paragraphs), an optional button label and photo |
| **Passes intro** | The heading and short blurb above your pass prices, plus the contact email in your page footer |
| **Photo gallery** | Square photos shown on your home and teacher pages. Click **+ Add** to upload several at once; the **×** removes one |
| **Social links** | Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube links for your page footer. Leave any blank to hide it |

If a field's text looks garbled (usually from pasting text that was already garbled somewhere else), a warning appears at the top naming the field — retype it before saving.
