# Plan & billing

**Settings → Plan & billing** is what CalaStudio charges your studio for using the platform. It's completely separate from what your members pay you: member payments go to your own Stripe account (see **Stripe setup**), while your CalaStudio plan is billed by CalaStudio. Only **Admin** can open it.

## The plans

- **Pay as you go**: a percentage of each card payment, worked out on what's left after Stripe's processing fee. No card needed and no fixed fee.
- **Monthly**: a fixed amount each month.
- **Annual**: a fixed amount each year.

The tab shows your rates in your studio's currency.

## GST

CalaStudio is run by Travel Engine, which charges GST to studios in Australia:

- **Monthly and Annual** prices **include GST**, e.g. "$45 a month incl. GST".
- **Pay as you go** has **GST added on top of the fee**. For example, a member pays $20 by card and Stripe's fee is $0.64. At 5%, CalaStudio's fee is 5% of $19.36 = $0.97, plus $0.10 GST, so $1.07 in total.

The Pay as you go fee comes out of each card payment automatically. It's first taken on the full amount, then trimmed back to the after-Stripe amount a moment later, once Stripe reports its fee; the difference goes back to your Stripe balance. If you refund a member, CalaStudio refunds the same share of its fee, including its GST. Stripe keeps its own processing fee.

Studios outside Australia aren't charged GST. You choose a plan when you sign up (the **Plan** step of onboarding) and can change it here at any time.

## Your plan

The top card shows the plan you're on and its price. For Monthly and Annual it also shows the subscription status and your **next billing date**.

If a payment fails, the status shows **Payment overdue** with a red message. Replace your card (below) to keep your plan.

## Payment card

Monthly and Annual are paid by card. Click **Add a card** (or **Replace card**) and enter it in the secure form. Card details go straight to Stripe; CalaStudio never sees them. You need a saved card before you can switch to Monthly or Annual.

## Switching plans

In **Change plan**, click **Switch to …** under the plan you want and confirm.

**Leaving Monthly or Annual never happens part-way through a period you've paid for.** If you're on Monthly until Sat 21 Nov and switch to Pay as you go, you stay on Monthly until 21 Nov and the switch happens then. Until that date:

- the top card says **Switching to Pay as you go on Sat 21 Nov**, and shows **Ends** instead of the next billing date
- the plan you're moving to is marked **From Sat 21 Nov**
- **Keep Monthly** cancels the switch. You stay on Monthly as if nothing happened, with no new subscription and no extra charge.

Moving from Pay as you go to Monthly or Annual starts the subscription on your saved card.

You're never charged twice for the same time. If CalaStudio staff end your Monthly or Annual plan early and you start one again before the period you'd paid for is over, the new plan begins with no charge and your first payment is taken when that period ends. Switching between Monthly and Annual works the same way.

Every plan change is recorded in the **Activity log**.

## Invoices

The **Invoices** list has every invoice from Travel Engine (CalaStudio), newest first. Click **PDF** to download one.

- **Monthly and Annual**: one invoice per billing period.
- **Pay as you go**: one invoice per month, listing that month's booking fees. It appears after the month ends.

Australian studios get **tax invoices** showing Travel Engine's ABN and the GST, so you can claim the GST back on your BAS.

## Questions about your bill

Contact CalaStudio support. CalaStudio staff can see your plan and, when agreed with you, adjust your rate. Any adjustment is recorded with a reason in your Activity log.
