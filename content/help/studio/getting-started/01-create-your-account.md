# Create your account

Getting started with CalaStudio takes less than 2 minutes.

## Sign up

1. Go to [calastudio.app](https://calastudio.app) and click **Start free**
2. Enter your email address and a password (minimum 8 characters)
3. Click **Create account**

You'll receive a verification email immediately.

## Verify your email

Open the email from CalaStudio and click the **Verify email** link. This confirms your address and opens the setup wizard.

> If you don't see the email, check your spam folder. The email comes from notifications@calastudio.app.

## What's next?

After verifying your email you'll be taken directly to the setup wizard — a 6-step process that gets your studio live in under 30 minutes.

See: [The onboarding wizard →](/help/studio/getting-started/02-onboarding-wizard)
