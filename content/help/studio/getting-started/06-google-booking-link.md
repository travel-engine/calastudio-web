# Add your booking link to Google

When someone searches for your studio on Google or Google Maps, they see your **Google Business Profile**: your address, opening hours, photos and reviews. You can add your CalaStudio schedule to it as a booking link, so people go straight from Google to booking a class.

It's free, takes a few minutes, and doesn't need anything from CalaStudio.

## Before you start

- **You need a verified Google Business Profile.** If you don't have one, create it at [business.google.com](https://business.google.com). Google won't let you add links until it's verified.
- **Copy your schedule link.** Use your schedule page, not your home page, so people land right on the classes:

  `https://{your-studio}.calastudio.app/schedule`

  For example, Yoga with Ravi uses `https://yoga-with-ravi.calastudio.app/schedule`. See **Getting started → Your web address**.

## Add the link

1. Search Google for your studio's name while you're signed in to the Google account that manages your profile. Or open [business.google.com](https://business.google.com).
2. In the panel for managing your profile, select **Bookings**. In the Google Maps app, select **Edit profile**, then the booking option.
3. Select **Add link**, or **Add another link** if one is already there.
4. Paste your schedule link and select **Save**.

If you have more than one booking link, choose **Set preferred link** and pick your CalaStudio one, so it's the one people see first.

Google checks the link before showing it, so it may not appear straight away.

## More than one location?

Google asks for each location's link to go to a page for that location, not a general one. Each location usually has its own Business Profile. Use your schedule link with that location chosen, which shows only its classes:

`https://{your-studio}.calastudio.app/schedule?location=Your%20Location%20Name`

The easiest way to get this link is to open your schedule, tap that location's filter, and copy the address from your browser. Spaces in the name appear as `%20`.

## Things Google won't accept

- A link to Instagram, Facebook or another social media page, a link shortener (like bit.ly) or an app store
- A page where people can't actually book

Your CalaStudio schedule meets Google's rules: it's a page just for your studio, and people can book on it.

## Already listed with a booking provider?

If another booking system you used before still appears on your Google profile, you can't remove it in Google's settings. Ask that provider to take it down; Google gives them 5 business days. Until then, set your CalaStudio link as your **preferred link**.
