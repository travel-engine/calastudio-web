# The onboarding wizard

The setup wizard gets your studio live in under 30 minutes. It has 6 steps — each with one job. You can click through in about 10 minutes if you accept all the defaults.

## Step 1 — Studio basics

Enter your studio name and your first name. Your country, currency, and timezone are auto-detected from your browser — just confirm they're correct and move on.

## Step 2 — First session

Create your first session. Give it a name, set a day and time, and choose whether it's in-person or online. Everything can be changed later.

**Tip:** Don't overthink this step. The goal is to have one session on your booking page so it doesn't look empty when you share the link.

## Step 3 — Pricing

Set your drop-in price for the session you just created. This is the price a member pays to book a single session without a pass.

Set it to **$0.00** if you want to run a free trial class.

## Step 4 — Connect payments

Connect your Stripe account so members can pay by card, Apple Pay, or Google Pay. Members pay you directly, into your own Stripe account; CalaStudio never holds your money. What CalaStudio charges depends on your plan — see [Plan and billing](/help/studio/settings/03-plan-and-billing).

**Don't have a Stripe account?** Click **Skip for now** — your booking page will go live in demo mode and you can connect Stripe later.

## Step 5 — You're live!

Your booking page is live at `{your-studio}.calastudio.app`. Copy the link and share it with your students.

## What to do next

After the wizard, your dashboard shows a checklist of optional next steps:
- Add your studio logo and description
- Create more sessions
- Set up class passes and memberships
- Set your cancellation policy
- Invite your team

None of these are required. They're available when you're ready.
