# Put CalaStudio on your phone

You and your team can add the CalaStudio dashboard to your phone's home screen. It gets a green **C** icon, opens full-screen like an app, and goes straight to your dashboard: today's classes, rosters and check-ins in one tap. There's nothing to download from the App Store or Google Play.

The quickest way: on the dashboard **Home** page, tap **Install app** (Android) or **Show me how** (iPhone) on the "Put CalaStudio on your home screen" card.

## On an Android phone (Chrome)

1. Open **calastudio.app/dashboard** in Chrome and sign in.
2. Tap **Install app** on the Home page. Otherwise, tap the **⋮** menu at the top right, then **Install** (some phones say **Add to Home screen**).
3. Tap **Install** to confirm.

## On an iPhone or iPad (Safari)

1. Open **calastudio.app/dashboard** in Safari and sign in.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up) in Safari's toolbar.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add**.

## Good to know

- **Sign in once inside the app.** On iPhone especially, the app keeps its own sign-in, separate from Safari. Tick **Keep me signed in on this device** so you're not asked again every day.
- **One app for every studio.** If you work at more than one CalaStudio studio, switch between them inside the app as you do on the website.
- **Members use a different app.** Your members install your studio's own booking site, with your logo (see *Add your studio to your home screen* in the member help).
- **On a computer:** Chrome and Edge also offer **Install app**, which gives the dashboard its own window.
- **Removing it:** press and hold the icon, then choose **Remove** or **Uninstall**.
- **Don't want to see the suggestion?** Tap **✕** on the card. It won't show again in that browser for 30 days.
