# Your web address

Your booking page lives at **`{your-studio}.calastudio.app`** — for example, Yoga with Ravi is at `yoga-with-ravi.calastudio.app`. Every page of your public site, and every teacher's landing page (`{your-studio}.calastudio.app/teachers/{their-name}`), sits under that address.

## Where it comes from

The address is made from your studio name when you first sign up: lowercase, with spaces turned into hyphens.

## Renaming your studio

You can change your studio's name any time in **Settings → Studio profile**. The new name shows everywhere your name appears — your booking page, page titles, emails and receipts.

Your web address isn't something you edit in Settings. It's in your links, QR codes, search results and social media bios, so it's kept separate from the name.

## Changing your web address

If you need a different address (for example after a rebrand), contact CalaStudio support. Before you do, think about everywhere the old address is printed or shared — flyers, QR codes, Instagram bio, Google Business profile — as those will need updating.
