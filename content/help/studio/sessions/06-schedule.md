# Schedule

**Schedule** in the sidebar is a calendar view of every occurrence in your studio — the week-by-week list of actual bookable dates, alongside a month overview of which days have classes on them.

## Week view

The main panel shows one week at a time, Monday to Sunday, each day listing its classes by time along with how full they are (e.g. `5/12`) — green if there's room, red once it's full. Use the **‹** and **›** buttons to move to the previous or next week, and **Today** (top right) to jump straight back to the current week.

Click any class to open its occurrence page — the same page you'd reach from **Bookings** or **Dashboard**'s "Next up" list — where you can mark attendance, add a student, edit the occurrence, or cancel it. See **Managing bookings** for what's on that page.

## Month calendar

The panel on the right shows the current month at a glance. Any day with at least one scheduled class is highlighted green. Use its own **‹** / **›** buttons to move a month at a time.

Click a day in the calendar and the week view jumps to the week containing it — a quick way to get from "there's something on the 24th" to the actual list of what's booked that day.

## What shows up here

Only **scheduled** occurrences appear — cancelled ones don't show on the calendar or in the week list at all. If a session has no occurrences yet (a brand new session, or one waiting on tonight's generation run), its days won't be highlighted until an occurrence actually exists — see **Recurring sessions** for how occurrences get created.

What you can see is also role-scoped the same way as everywhere else: Admin, Super teacher, and Front desk see every class in the studio; Teacher and Basic teacher only see occurrences for sessions they're the instructor on. See **Team → Teacher roles** for the full breakdown.
