# Schedule

**Schedule** in the sidebar is a calendar view of every class in your studio — the week-by-week list of actual bookable dates, alongside a month overview of which days have classes on them. It's also the quickest way into any class's roster.

## Week view

The main panel shows one week at a time, Monday to Sunday. Each day lists its classes by start time with the venue, the teacher (or **No teacher assigned**), and how full it is — a bar plus a count like `5 / 12`. The bar turns amber when a class is almost full and red once it's full. Today's row is highlighted; a day with nothing on shows **No classes**, with an **Add one** link if you can create sessions.

Use the **‹** and **›** buttons to move to the previous or next week, and **This week** to jump straight back.

Click any class to open its roster — see **Managing bookings** for what you can do there: check people in, add a booking, edit the class, or cancel it.

## Month calendar

The panel on the right shows a month at a glance. A dot under a day means it has at least one class, and today is ringed. Use its own **‹** / **›** buttons to move a month at a time.

Click a day and the week view jumps to the week containing it — a quick way to get from "there's something on the 24th" to the actual list of what's booked that day.

## Cancelled classes

Cancelled classes are hidden by default. Tick **Show cancelled** (top right, with a count) to see them in the week view, clearly marked as cancelled.

## What shows up here

A session's classes are created as soon as it's saved, so its days get dots straight away. If a session's days aren't showing, its schedule produced no dates within the booking horizon — see **Recurring sessions** for how classes get created.

What you can see is also role-scoped the same way as everywhere else: Admin, Super teacher, and Front desk see every class in the studio; Teacher and Basic teacher only see classes for sessions they're the instructor on. See **Team → Teacher roles** for the full breakdown.

Times are shown in your studio's time zone (set in **Settings → Region & time zone**), not the time zone of the device you're using.
