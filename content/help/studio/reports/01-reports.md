# Reports

**Reports** in the sidebar shows how your studio is doing over a date range. **Admin** sees every tab; **Super teacher** sees **Attendance** only.

## Choosing a date range

Pick **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, **This month** or **Last month**, or click **Custom range** and choose start and end dates. The range you're looking at is shown above the **Reports** title, and every tab uses it.

## Overview

A one-page summary:

- **Revenue** — total sales, split into drop-ins and passes
- **Booked spots** — bookings in the range, and what share of available spots they filled
- **Attended** — how many people showed up, plus no-shows, and the show-up rate (attended out of attended plus no-shows, for classes that have run). Members and their guests count individually: a member who came with a guest who didn't is 1 attended and 1 no-show
- **New members** — sign-ups in the range, and how many active members you have in total
- **Active passes** — passes in use right now, and how many credits members haven't used yet

Below that:

- **Booked spots by day** — a bar per day, with your busiest day called out
- **How full classes are** — average fill by day of the week and start time. Darker green means fuller; a dashed cell means no class ran then
- **Class performance** — each session's number of classes, average booked against capacity, fill, attended and no-shows. Click a session to open it
- **Pass types** — how many of each pass have been sold, and how many are active now
- **Members to win back** — members who haven't booked recently. Click a name to open their profile, or **See all** for the full list on the Members tab

If there are no bookings in the range, the charts are replaced by a short message with **Try last 30 days** and **Copy booking page link**.

## The other tabs

| Tab | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Revenue** | Total revenue, drop-in, pass sales, refunds and net, plus every transaction in the range with its member, product, method and status |
| **Attendance** | Classes, bookings, attended, no-shows, average fill and show-up rate, the fill chart, and class performance |
| **Passes** | Active passes, passes sold, credits not yet used, passes expiring in the next 7 days, a table of pass types, and passes expiring in the next 30 days |
| **Members** | Active and new members, growth by month, members by type, members to win back, top members by attendance and spend, and anyone on an old or unsigned waiver |

## Exporting

Click **Export CSV** (top right) to download every transaction in the selected range as a spreadsheet — the same list as the Revenue tab. Admin only.

Money is always shown in your studio's currency.
