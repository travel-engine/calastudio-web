# Tax on receipts

If you're required to charge sales tax (GST, VAT, or similar), configure it once in Settings and it's applied automatically to every card payment from then on — no need to touch it per session or per booking.

## Setting it up

1. Go to **Settings**, under **Tax (GST/VAT)**
2. Add at least one rate under **Tax rate schedule** — click **+ Add rate** and enter the percentage and the date it takes effect. Adding a new rate automatically closes out whichever one was previously current, the day before the new one starts
3. Fill in **Tax label** (e.g. "GST" or "VAT") and **Tax registration number** (your ABN, VAT number, or equivalent) — both appear on receipts
4. Tick **Charge tax on bookings**

Tax can't be turned on until at least one rate is configured — the checkbox is disabled until then.

## What members see

A booking paid by card includes a receipt emailed automatically, breaking out the subtotal, the tax amount, and your tax label and registration number. Whichever rate is in effect on the day of the transaction is the one applied — a rate you add today with a future start date won't affect anything until that date arrives.

## Changing your rate later

Add a new row to the schedule with the new rate and its start date, rather than editing the existing one — that way past receipts stay accurate to whatever rate was actually in effect when they were issued.
