# Tax on receipts

If you're required to charge sales tax (GST, VAT, or similar), configure it once in Settings and it's applied automatically to every card payment from then on — no need to touch it per session or per booking.

## Setting it up

1. Go to **Settings** and find the **Tax (GST)** card
2. Under **Rates**, click **Add a rate** and enter the percentage and the date it starts. Adding a new rate automatically closes out whichever one was previously current, the day before the new one starts. The rate in effect today is tagged **Current**
3. Fill in **Tax name** (e.g. "GST" or "VAT") and **ABN / tax number** (your ABN, VAT number, or equivalent) — both appear on receipts
4. Turn on **Charge tax on bookings**
5. Click **Save** on the Tax card

Add a rate before turning tax on — the card reminds you if there isn't one yet. Rates are saved as soon as you add them; the name, number and on/off switch are saved with the card's **Save** button.

## What members see

A booking paid by card includes a receipt emailed automatically, breaking out the subtotal, the tax amount, and your tax label and registration number. Whichever rate is in effect on the day of the transaction is the one applied — a rate you add today with a future start date won't affect anything until that date arrives.

## Changing your rate later

Click **Add a future rate** and enter the new rate and its start date, rather than editing the existing one — that way past receipts stay accurate to whatever rate was actually in effect when they were issued.
