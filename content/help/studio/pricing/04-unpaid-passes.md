# Unpaid passes and payment links

Sometimes a member takes a pass now and pays later: they'll bring cash next week, they're paying by bank transfer, or you've agreed a special price. You can add the pass straight away, so they can book with it immediately, and record the payment when it comes in.

## Add a pass as unpaid

1. Open the member from **Members**
2. Under **Passes**, click **Add pricing option**
3. Choose the pass and, if needed, a start date
4. Under **Payment**, choose **Not paid yet (member pays later)**
5. Optional: tick **Email them a payment link now** to send them a link they can pay by card
6. Click **Add pass**

The pass works straight away. On the member's page it shows an **Unpaid** strip with the amount owed until it's paid.

The other **Payment** choices are:

- **No payment (comp / promo):** a free pass, nothing is owed
- **Cash**, **Bank transfer**, **Credit card**, **Debit card**, **Gift card:** they've already paid, and the payment is recorded in their orders

## Give a special price

Admins and Super teachers can change the price when adding a pass, for example a seniors offer. Type the price in **Price**, then add a **Reason for the different price**, such as "Seniors offer, age 72". The reason is required and is shown on the pass and in the activity log.

Leave **Price** blank to charge the pass's usual price.

## When they pay you in person: Mark as paid

1. Open the member and find the pass with the **Unpaid** strip
2. Click **Mark as paid**
3. Choose how they paid: **Cash**, **Bank transfer**, **Credit card (in person)**, **Debit card (in person)** or **Gift card**
4. Click **Record … paid**

The strip disappears and the payment appears in their **Orders** and in **Reports → Revenue**.

If the member is part-way through paying online at that moment, you'll see "The member is paying online right now". Check again in a few minutes, so the same pass isn't paid twice.

## Send a payment reminder

Click **Send payment reminder** on the unpaid pass. The member gets an email with a **Pay** button. They can pay by card **without signing in**, and the pass is marked paid automatically.

After sending, the link is also shown with a **Copy link** button, so you can text it to them. **Send reminder again** sends a fresh email any time.

Card payment links need your studio to be connected to Stripe. If it isn't, use **Mark as paid** instead.

## What the member sees

In **My account → My passes**, an unpaid pass shows **Unpaid** with the amount and a **Pay** button, with the note "You can already book with this pass." See **Paying for a pass the studio added** in the member help.

## Who can do this

Admins and Super teachers can add passes, change the price, mark passes paid and send payment reminders. See **Team → Teacher roles**.
