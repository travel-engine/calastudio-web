# Memberships

A **membership** sells a period of access, such as unlimited classes for $45 a week, charged automatically until the member cancels. A **pass** sells a number of credits instead (see **Pricing → Passes**).

## Create a plan

Go to **Memberships** → **New membership**. Set the price, how often it renews (every week, month, or a number of days), how many sessions a member can book each period (or unlimited), and which session types it covers. Only **Admin** can create or edit plans.

Members join from your **Pricing** page or from the booking panel, choose a start date, and pay the first period up front. Their card is saved for renewals.

## Renewals and failed payments

Each renewal is charged early in the morning on the first day of the new period. If the card is declined, the membership shows **Payment overdue**, the member is emailed a link to pay, and the charge is retried after 1, 3 and 5 days. If the last retry fails, the membership is cancelled and you're emailed.

## Members' memberships

The **Members** tab on the Memberships page lists everyone's memberships. Open one to see its payments and history, or to:

- **Cancel** it today (with an optional refund for the unused days), or at the end of the current period
- **Change the price** for that member only (Admin)

To give someone a membership for free, open their member page and use **Give membership**. Only **Admin** and **Super teacher** roles can manage members' memberships.
