# Early bird pricing

Give people a reason to book early: a workshop can have a lower **early bird** price until a date you choose, then the full price. You can also limit the early bird price to the first few spots.

Early bird is for one-off sessions, such as workshops. Weekly classes can't have it.

## Set it up

1. Open the session from **Sessions** (or create a new one) and make sure it's **One-off**
2. In **Pricing**, set the **Drop-in price**: this is the full price
3. Tick **Offer an early bird price**
4. Enter the **Early bird price**. It must be lower than the full price
5. Choose when it **Ends on**, and **At** what time. This is your studio's own time, whatever device you're using. It must be before the workshop starts
6. Optional: **Limit to the first … spots**. Leave it blank to offer early bird to everyone who books before the end date
7. Save

Underneath, the form tells you in plain words what members will pay, for example: "Members pay A$120.00 until Sat 28 Nov 11:59 pm or until 3 early bird spots are taken, then A$150.00."

To stop offering it, untick **Offer an early bird price** and save.

## What members see

On your timetable and when booking, the workshop shows **Early bird A$120.00** with the full price crossed out, and a nudge underneath:

- "Early bird until 1 Dec, 11:59 pm", then "Early bird ends in 3 days" as the date gets close
- "Only 3 early bird spots left" when there's a spot limit and only a few remain

When the date passes or the early bird spots are taken, it switches to the full price on its own.

The price is fixed when someone starts paying. If they start checking out at 11:58 pm they keep the early bird price, and a place is held for them while they pay.

## Memberships and passes

If a member's membership covers the workshop (the membership includes workshops, or you've ticked it under **Who can book**), they book at no charge, so early bird doesn't apply to them. To make a workshop pay-only, set **Booking access** to **Restricted**, tick **Also allow drop-in payment**, and leave the passes and memberships unticked (or tick only the memberships that should cover it).

## Booking someone in yourself

When you add someone to the workshop and choose **Paid to the studio**, leave the price blank to charge whatever applies at that moment: early bird while it's on, otherwise the full price. Admins and Super teachers can enter a different price with a reason, for example to honour early bird after the deadline. See **Sessions → Managing bookings**.

## Seeing how it sold

Each booking records which price was paid. The class roster shows **Early bird** or **Special price** next to the payment, receipts say "(Early bird)", and **Reports → Revenue** shows early bird, full-price and special-price totals under **Booking prices**.
